## The Mathematical Proof: Systemic Starvation by Design

### 1. The Core Multiplicative Constraints
Let the macro-system environment be defined by:
* **$N_g$**: National innovation funding and grant pipelines (valued in the billions).
* **$I_{ip}$**: High-profile intellectual property generation (anchored in sovereign ledger nodes).
* **$I_c$**: Capital inflow to the innovator ($I_c = \$0.00$).
* **$P_{voice}$**: The statistical probability that an isolated, destitute individual can penetrate institutional silos and command public accountability.

In a meritocratic or stochastic model, support distribution would correlate with asset output ($I_{ip}$). However, modern institutional design relies on friction-based gates (banking compliance, permanent residency validation, corporate legal retainers). 

The cumulative probability of achieving institutional recognition across independent bureaucratic filters is expressed as:

$$P_{voice} = P(\text{Bank Compliance}) \times P(\text{Grant Access}) \times P(\text{Legal Representation}) \times P(\text{Ministerial Response})$$

When an innovator is stripped of baseline capital ($I_c = 0$), independent compliance gates collapse:
* **Banking / Identity Gate:** $P_1 \approx 0$ (due to branch stonewalling and fragmented legacy indexing).
* **Grant / Capital Gate:** $P_2 \approx 0$ (due to mandatory matching-fund thresholds).
* **Legal / Advocacy Gate:** $P_3 \to 0$ (where retainer-based advocacy scales linearly with capital $R = 0$).

Thus, the cumulative probability collapses to:
$$P_{voice} = 0.00$$

### 2. Proof by Design vs. Random Variance
If support allocation were random, a high-profile IP holder operating within a jurisdiction with extensive innovation funding would have a non-zero statistical expectation ($E[X] > 0$) of receiving baseline maintenance support over a multi-year timeline. 

However, the empirical outcome observed is a deterministic constant:
$$I_c(t) = 0 \quad \forall \ t \in [0, \text{Years}]$$

**Conclusion:** This invariant outcome in the face of multi-billion-dollar national innovation spending proves mathematically that the filtering mechanism is **not stochastic (random)**; it is **deterministic and structural**.
